# Claude Opus 5 in the registry, and GPT-5.6 named by variant

- **Date:** 2026-08-19
- **Type:** feature
- **Scope:** `registry`, `skills`

[中文版](2026-08-19-opus-5-and-gpt-5.6-sol.zh.md)

## What changed

- `claude-opus-5` was added to the registry for the official Anthropic API and, as
  `anthropic/claude-opus-5`, for OpenRouter. Both route through the existing `claude-5`
  client; no client change was needed.
- The official GPT-5.6 registry entry was renamed from the bare `gpt-5.6` alias to the
  concrete `gpt-5.6-sol`, matching the OpenRouter entry and the `gpt-5.6-terra` /
  `gpt-5.6-luna` siblings. The bare `gpt-5.6` spelling still routes to the GPT-5.6 client.
- `README.md` now shows `gpt-5.6-sol` in the supported-model table and in both quickstart
  examples.
- Both `skills/*/reference/models.md` gained the missing GPT 5.6 rows (official and
  OpenRouter) and list `claude-opus-5` and `claude-sonnet-5` alongside `claude-fable-5`
  in the Claude 5 row.

## Registry metadata

`claude-opus-5` is registered with text and image input, text output, and a
1,000,000-token context window. Pricing comes from
https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing: $5 input, $0.50 cached input
(the cache-hit rate), and $25 output per million tokens, on both the official entry and
the OpenRouter entry, whose rates `GET https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models` reports as
identical.

`gpt-5.6-sol` keeps the rates the renamed entry already carried, confirmed against
OpenAI's published pricing: $5 input, $0.50 cached input, $30 output per million tokens.
