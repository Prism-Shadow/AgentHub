# Support Gemini 3.8 Flash

- **Date:** 2026-09-03
- **Type:** feat
- **Scope:** `registry`, `gemini3_7`, `docs`
- **PR:** [#199](https://github.com/Prism-Shadow/agenthub/pull/199)

[中文版](2026-09-03-gemini-3.8-flash.zh.md)

## What changed

- Added `gemini-3.8-flash` to the model registry on the official Google endpoint: text, image,
  video and audio in, text out, a 1,048,576-token context window, and the launch price of
  0.75/3.75 per million tokens with cache hits at 0.075, in force through 2026-12-31.
- Excluded the 3.8 generation from the `minimal` thinking level, which it rejects with a 400 the
  way 3.7 does, so `thinking_level: none` clamps to `low` instead of being forwarded.
